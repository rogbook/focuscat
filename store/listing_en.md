## Description

You want to focus, but the phone keeps pulling you back.

FocusCat is simple. Choose a length, start, put the phone down.
Your cat stalks a butterfly on the screen while you work.

**Your cat hunts while you focus**
As the timer runs, your cat closes in on a butterfly. At first it just sits and watches, tail swishing. Then its eyes go wide as it takes aim, and it starts creeping closer. When time is up it snatches the butterfly with one paw. You can tell how far along you are from the cat's posture alone, without checking the clock.

**Your cat calls you when time is up**
When the session ends your cat meows until you tap the screen. Leave the phone face down and you still won't miss it.

**Leave the app and your focus breaks**
Switch to another app and the session is recorded as failed. There is a 25 second grace period for a mistapped notification or a short call, but stay away longer and your cat gives up waiting. That one rule is the whole app.

**Lofi plays while you focus**
A quiet loop runs in the background. One tap mutes it when you're somewhere you can't make noise.

**You decide the length**
Pick 15, 25, 45 or 60 minutes, or set your own from 1 minute up to 3 hours.

**A cat that grows with you**
Successful focus time adds up and your cat grows.

**No ads while you focus**
Ads appear only after a session ends. Nothing covers the screen while you're focusing.

No account, no sign-in, no purchases. Free.
