집중하는 동안 고양이가 사냥을 합니다.

시간이 흐를수록 고양이가 나비를 사냥합니다. 지켜보다가 눈이 커지며 조준하고, 살금살금 다가가다, 시간이 끝나면 앞발로 잡습니다. 남은 시간을 보지 않아도 고양이 자세만으로 얼마나 왔는지 알 수 있습니다.

## English (App Store)

Your cat goes hunting while you focus.

• As time passes, the cat hunts a butterfly — it watches, its eyes go wide as it takes aim, it creeps closer, and when the timer ends it catches the butterfly with one paw
• You can tell how far along you are from the cat's posture alone, without checking the clock
• When your focus session ends, the cat meows. Tap the screen to stop it
• New app icon

## English (Google Play · under 500 characters)

Your cat goes hunting while you focus.

As time passes, the cat hunts a butterfly. It watches, its eyes go wide as it takes aim, it creeps closer, and when the timer ends it catches the butterfly with one paw. You can tell how far along you are from the cat's posture alone.

When your session ends, the cat meows. Tap the screen to stop it.

New app icon, too.
